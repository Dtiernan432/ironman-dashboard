Plan rules

Late-start: no planned training before 09:00.
Work: Tue/Wed 16:30–21:30. Monday meeting 20:00–21:00.
Strength: three reserved hypertrophy slots; actual lifts are not prescribed.
Testing: no invented FTP. First test establishes the bike zones.

Race model

Working sub-12 model: 1:30 swim + 5:20 bike + 4:55 run + ~10 min transitions = 11:55. This is a target model, not a prediction. Actual bike watts and run pace are locked after testing and race-specific validation.

The plan prioritises bike development because you want a slower swim / faster bike strategy, while keeping enough run frequency to protect the marathon.

Race rehearsal

At ~5:20 bike, 90 g/h is roughly 480 g CHO over the bike. At ~4:55 run, 70 g/h is roughly 340 g. Build gradually and test exact products repeatedly.

Sweat-rate test

Record pre/post body mass, fluid consumed and urine on a long session. Use this with conditions and product sodium to individualise fluid and sodium.

300 W is not prescribed now. If FTP eventually supports ~300 W at an appropriate Ironman intensity and you validate it on long race-specific rides without compromising the run, it can become a race target.

Methodology

Dan Lorang

Technique → speed/VO₂ → strength/endurance → economisation. The final ~8 weeks become increasingly race-specific, with emphasis on Ironman-level economy and energy management.

Dan Plews

Use HRV as a readiness trend alongside sleep, motivation and subjective feel. A single low reading does not dictate the day; sustained deterioration can justify backing off.

Lance Watson

Every workout has a role. Weekly progression is built around the athlete's life schedule and developmental needs rather than treating every day as hard.

Kristian Blummenfelt

Borrow the large low-intensity base and controlled threshold concept. Do not copy professional volume or double-threshold frequency into an age-group 20–28 h plan.

Key principle: most endurance time stays easy; hard work is concentrated and purposeful. The exact distribution changes by phase.
